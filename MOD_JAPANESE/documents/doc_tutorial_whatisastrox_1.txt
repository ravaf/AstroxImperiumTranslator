<size='25'><color=cyan>What is Astrox Imperium</color></size>

Simply put, a game that I wanted to play, but could not find. Astrox is like many space games. It has all the basic stuff you would expect to find in a 3d space sim. The game doesn’t do anything radical or groundbreaking, quite the opposite actually. So what makes Astrox special?

If you are reading this, you probably love space games as much as I do. The problem I found was that of all the space games out there, none of them offered the kind of gameplay I was looking for. I came to realize that it was quite possible that none of them ever would. I came onto an idea where you could play a space game, but you would control and set the parameters of how the game would play and feel.

Astrox has evolved to become a very versatile game, with many options and settings that allow you to create an experience closer to what you are looking for in a space sim. The sandbox will provide you the options to tailor the universe to your liking. The in game options will allow you to adjust how the game is played.

You have the ability to turn on and off certain mechanics of the game. You can adjust how the game looks and responds to the commands you give. Because of this feature, it may take some time to figure out how it all works and what you prefer. If you discover that you want the game to do something that is currently not available, be sure to visit the steam forums and drop a suggestion. I am very receptive to player input, and credit many of the great parts about Astrox to you all, the players.
